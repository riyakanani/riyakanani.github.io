Family Luggage Tags: Laser Engraving

Created for my laser cutting and 3D printing class at Carnegie Mellon University, Fall 2026.

Key Words: Laser engraving · Photo engraving · Wood and acrylic · Adobe Illustrator · Personalized design

What I Built:

I designed a set of personalized luggage tags for my family, one for each of us.

Photo Engraving:

The backs of the wooden tags carry engraved family photos, prepared in Illustrator so the tones translate into the wood.

Mehndi Design:

Some tags have a peacock mehndi pattern instead, engraved as fine line work.

Acrylic Pieces:

I also tried engraving on acrylic: a clear oval with a photo and a black star-shaped keychain.

Tools Used:

• Laser cutter
• Adobe Illustrator
• Wood and acrylic sheets


Overview:

My first laser cutting project: learning how the laser handles photos, line art and different materials, and turning that into something my family can actually use when we travel.
